PERSONALITY

MBTI & Zodiac traits

Ideally you'd get me without labels. But if coordinates help you tune in faster, these two sections are the parts that hold up reasonably well.

1. MBTI · INTJ-A

INTJ is often called the "Architect": good at abstracting messy information into structure, then reasoning several steps ahead. The real differentiator is the -A (Assertive) suffix — stable emotions, pressure-resistant, low self-consumption; once the direction is set, short-term noise rarely derails it.

Traits people often get right:

  • Systems over patches. For recurring problems, the first instinct isn't to fix this one instance — it's to build a system that fixes the whole class automatically.
  • Independent judgment. No blind deference to authority, no shortcuts — conclusions enter the knowledge base only after my own verification.
  • Blunt honesty. Direct feedback, aimed at the issue not the person — saves everyone the bandwidth of guessing minds.
  • Long-termism. Watching the version number five years out, not this week's noise.

Honest about the downsides too: socializing drains energy and needs solitude to recharge; severely allergic to “good enough” and “everyone does it”; occasionally “read but not replied” while deep in thought — that's usually the system compiling in the background, not coldness.

2. Zodiac · Aquarius (Jan 20 – Feb 18)

Aquarius is an air sign ruled by Uranus — the planet that keeps asking “why must we do it the old way”. Its base tone: independent, forward-looking, anti-convention, holding the world at a rational distance.

  • No blind following. Authority and convention enter a review queue by default; only a sound logic gets them through.
  • Future-oriented. Less “what's trending now”, more “what will this look like in five years”.
  • Freedom matters. Physical and mental autonomy are both high priority.
  • Idealistic but rational. Cares about humanity as a whole, yet may not appear in the team-building photo — the detachment is an observer's vantage, not coldness.

Stacked on INTJ, it becomes “wants clear structure but refuses the beaten path”: enjoys designing rules and breaking them — provided the new rule is better.

Zodiac is for fun; MBTI is a map, not a boundary. What truly defines a person is every choice and every iteration.
